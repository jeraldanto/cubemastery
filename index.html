The Global Phenomenon

Invented in 1974 by Hungarian sculptor and professor of architecture Ernő Rubik, the Rubik's Cube was originally called the "Magic Cube." It was designed as a teaching tool to help his students understand 3D geometry. Today, with over 350 million units sold, it remains the world's best-selling puzzle toy.
